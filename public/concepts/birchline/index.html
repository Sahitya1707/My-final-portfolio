What happens at your first visit

Your first appointment is an hour long, so there's time to listen properly. You'll leave knowing what's going on and what to do about it.

  1. First 15 minutes

    We talk

    What happened, what makes it better or worse, and what you want to get back to.

  2. Next 20 minutes

    We assess

    Gentle movement and strength tests to find what's causing the problem.

  3. Next 15 minutes

    First treatment

    Hands-on treatment and exercises, so you start feeling the difference today.

  4. Last 10 minutes

    Your plan

    A clear plan, a few exercises for home (sent by email), and an honest estimate of how many visits you'll need.

Book an assessment

Tell us a little about what's going on and when suits you. The front desk will call to confirm a time.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. In Ontario you can book a physiotherapist directly. Some insurance plans ask for a referral before they reimburse, so check your plan.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in. For a knee or hip, shorts are useful; for a shoulder, a tank top or loose t-shirt.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the problem. After your assessment your physiotherapist will give you an honest estimate and a plan you can do at home, and you decide how often to come back.

What's the cancellation policy?

Please give 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations are charged half the session fee, and the first one is always waived.

Not for emergencies. If you have chest pain, sudden weakness, numbness, or a serious injury, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.
