02 · Right now

Water where it shouldn't be? Do this first.

Four steps that limit the damage while you wait for a plumber.

Then call 555-010-0167
  1. Turn off the waterFind the main shut-off where the water line comes into the house, usually in the basement by the meter. Turn it clockwise until it stops. For one fixture, use the small valve under the sink or behind the toilet.
  2. Keep electricity away from waterIf water is near outlets, appliances or the electrical panel, stay out of it. Switch off that circuit only if you can reach the panel while standing somewhere dry.
  3. Open the tapsOpen the lowest tap in the house to drain what's left in the pipes and take pressure off the leak.
  4. Take photosPhotograph the leak and any damage before you clean up. Your insurer will ask for them.

05 · Questions

Common questions

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No separate call-out fee. The first half hour of labour is included in every starting price, and you approve the price before any work begins.

Do you work evenings and weekends?

Yes. Phones are answered 7 am to 9 pm every day, and evening and weekend visits cost the same as weekdays.

How long is your work guaranteed?

Labour is guaranteed for 12 months. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, which we register for you.

My water heater is old. Repair or replace?

Tank heaters usually last 8 to 12 years. If yours is in that range and leaking from the tank itself, replacing it is normally better value. If it's a valve or element, a repair is often enough. We'll tell you which, and why.
