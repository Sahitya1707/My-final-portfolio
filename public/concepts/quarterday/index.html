About Quarterday

“A quarter day was the day rent fell due. We named the bakery after the old idea of a day that comes round, reliably, whatever else happens.”

Quarterday is two bakers and a coffee machine in a former hardware shop on Mill Street. We bake through the night, open the doors at half past six, and close when the bread runs out or at three, whichever comes first.

We buy flour from a mill in the province, keep the menu short, and would rather sell out than throw bread away. Anything left at closing goes to the community fridge on Bridge Road.

We also bake for a handful of local cafés and grocers. If you'd like a regular order, call us after 2 pm.
